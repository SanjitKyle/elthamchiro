Meet our podiatrist

Ina Farrelly

Podiatrist · Dip.PG, B.Sc, MRCPod · Society of Tissue Viability Trustee · HCPC Registered

Ina has been in the profession for over 20 years and qualified in New Zealand. She believes podiatry is about much more than treating foot problems — it’s about helping people stay healthy, active and independent, and continuing to enjoy the things that matter to them.

She has developed a particular passion for biomechanics and the relationship between foot health, movement and overall wellbeing. Her approach is centred on understanding the whole person, identifying the factors contributing to their concerns and providing individualised care that supports both recovery and long-term health.

Ina’s career has included working within the NHS and as part of the East London Wound Team, which later became Accelerate CIC, supporting patients with complex wounds and lymphoedema. Her clinical experience spans biomechanics, musculoskeletal podiatry, wound care and footwear. She has also been involved in research, publications and professional education, and is proud to have served as a Trustee of the Society of Tissue Viability. She wrote Navigating the Healthcare System to support people in dealing with a system that can be difficult to understand.

In private practice, Ina particularly values taking the time to get to know patients, listen to their concerns and work together to find the best approach for them. She aims to provide thoughtful, friendly and personalised care, helping people understand their foot health, prevent future problems and maintain comfort, confidence and mobility.

Ultimately, Ina’s goal is to help you feel confident in your feet and enjoy life to the fullest.
