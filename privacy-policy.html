Your information

Privacy Policy

How information may be handled when you visit this website or contact our clinic.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers this website only; clinical care and patient records are subject to separate privacy information provided by the clinic.

Information you provide

This website displays contact details so you can contact reception by telephone. The contact enquiry form currently displays an on-page notice and does not transmit the entered information to the clinic. Do not include health or other sensitive information in a website form. If you contact the clinic by phone, reception will handle the information you choose to provide for responding to your enquiry and arranging care.

Technical information and third parties

When you load this website, your browser may connect to third-party providers used to deliver resources such as fonts, icons, mapping or page styles. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, and the requested resource. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy notices. The website host may also process technical request information to deliver and protect the site.

How information is used

Information provided directly to reception is used to respond to you and handle your enquiry. Technical information may be processed by the hosting or resource providers to provide, secure and troubleshoot their services. This static website does not itself provide online appointment booking or submit its contact form to a clinic database.

Sharing and retention

Information you provide to reception may be shared where needed to respond to your request, provide services, or meet legal obligations. Retention of clinical and enquiry records is handled under the clinic’s applicable record-keeping practices. For retention details about information you provided directly to the clinic, contact reception.

Your rights and questions

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of personal information, or to raise an objection. To ask how information you provided to the clinic is handled, call 020 8859 2223 or use the contact page. You may also contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office for information about your data protection rights.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or information practices change.

Questions about privacy?

Contact reception about information you have provided directly to the clinic.
