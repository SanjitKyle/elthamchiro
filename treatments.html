01 / Chiropractic
Specialist treatment

Chiropractic Care

The clinic’s chiropractors focus on the musculoskeletal system, taking a whole-body view. People enquire about concerns such as migraines and neck-related headaches, neck and shoulder pain, arm and hand problems, and low-back, hip or knee pain.

Your first appointment

The clinic describes a one-hour first appointment, usually including treatment, followed by 20-minute appointments.

Foot health & care

Podiatry & Foot Care

The podiatry team treats foot and lower-leg conditions, from routine nail and hard-skin care to ingrown toenails, verrucae, heel and big-toe problems, infections, and foot or ankle injuries. The clinic also describes care for foot complaints associated with diabetes and rheumatoid arthritis.

Routine foot and nail care
Biomechanics assessment and gait analysis
Custom foot orthoses and insoles

Osteopathy

The clinic describes osteopathy as a hands-on approach to assessing and caring for the body’s muscles, joints and connective tissues. Depending on the person and their needs, care may include manual techniques, stretching, massage, posture advice and exercises.

Movement & Sports Concerns

Sports injuries, sprains and strains are among the concerns people may discuss with a practitioner.
