This policy explains cookies and similar technologies in relation to the Eltham Chiropractic & Podiatry Clinic website.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies can store or access information on your device or help a service recognise requests.

Use on this website

The website’s current code does not set its own cookies or use a cookie-consent banner. It loads some resources from third-party providers, including fonts, icons, page styling and map resources. Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies, or receive technical data such as your IP address, depending on how their services work and your browser settings. Please refer to the relevant provider’s cookie and privacy information for details.

Managing cookies

You can review or delete cookies and change cookie settings through your browser. Blocking cookies may affect third-party features or resources. Browser settings do not necessarily prevent the transmission of basic technical information needed to request a web resource.

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy if website technologies change. For questions about the website, call reception on 020 8859 2223 or visit our contact page.
