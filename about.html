About your local clinic

Personal care, close to home in Eltham

Eltham Chiropractic & Podiatry is a multidisciplinary clinic offering chiropractic, osteopathy, podiatry and nutrition. Our team welcomes enquiries from people looking for support with musculoskeletal, foot and lower-limb concerns.

We are based at 10 Westmount Road, Eltham, London SE9 1JD. Contact reception for general questions about services and appointments.

Need Some Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions.

We understand that visiting a clinic for the first time can bring questions. Here are some helpful answers to get you started.

Still have questions?

Our reception team will be happy to help with general enquiries about our clinic and services.

Call 020 8859 2223
What services does your clinic offer?

We offer chiropractic, osteopathy, podiatry and nutrition services. Please contact reception for information about specific treatments and availability.

Where is the clinic located?

We are located at 10 Westmount Road, Eltham, London SE9 1JD. You can use the address to find directions on Google Maps.

How can I arrange an appointment?

You can call our reception team on 020 8859 2223 or use our contact page to enquire about arranging an appointment.

Do I need to know which treatment I need before visiting?

Not necessarily. You can contact reception to discuss your concerns and ask which service may be appropriate. Your practitioner can discuss your individual needs during your appointment.

Is parking available near the clinic?

Half-hour free parking is available on Westmount Road, with a ticket required. Please check local parking signs before leaving your vehicle.

What are your opening hours?

Opening hours vary depending on the service and day. Please refer to the opening times listed in our footer or contact reception for the latest appointment availability.

Can I ask about treatment prices before booking?

Yes. Please contact reception to enquire about fees for the service you are interested in before arranging an appointment.
